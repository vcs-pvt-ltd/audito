## Demo execution scenario

- Answer Questions 10 and 17 as **Non-compliant/No** and require corrective actions.
- Answer Questions 15, 19, and 23 as **Partially compliant**.
- Upload a sample wastewater report for Question 17 and a waste manifest for Question 20.
- Add an environmental incident remark to Question 12.
- Give the wastewater corrective action a short due date so it can later demonstrate overdue CAP behavior.
