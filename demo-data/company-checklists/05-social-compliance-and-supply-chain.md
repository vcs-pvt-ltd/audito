## Demo execution scenario

- Answer Questions 9, 12, and 15 as **No** and require corrective actions.
- Answer Questions 11, 16, and 19 as **Partially compliant**.

- Add a confidential-grievance observation as a remark to Question 12.
- Assign corrective actions to different responsible users to test Organization User visibility and CAP ownership.
